Therefore...  pianocheetah !

 - for keyboards and drums.  (electric ones)

 - song notes are squished to 1 per hand max and your hands stay put.

 - notes are shown aligned to your fingers - normal people notation.

 - you set the tempo (time variation) and note velocities (feel).

 - background tracks play along with you at your tempo and velocity.

 - you can play a song with your feel as haltingly as you like and
   the halts are gone when you play it back and save it.
