SHazTunez privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

SHazTunez is a music player for your own music files. It doesn't collect, store or send any personal data to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your music folder

When you first open the app you pick one folder (normally Music/tunez). The app can only see that folder and what's inside it. Inside that folder it:

  • plays your song files
  • renames a song's file when you change its rating, and deletes a song only when you tap delete
  • keeps a few small text files of its own: done.txt (songs already played this round), skip.txt (songs you skipped) and tunez_debug.log (a troubleshooting log)

All of this stays on your phone. You can remove the app's access to the folder at any time by uninstalling it.

Casting

If you cast to a Chromecast or other Google Cast device, the app sends the songs to that device directly over your own Wi-Fi network. Finding and talking to cast devices is handled by Google Play services, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Lyrics

The lyrics button opens a Google search in your browser for the current song's title and artist. That search is between you and Google.

Notifications

The app shows a notification while music is playing, so you can pause or skip from the lock screen. It doesn't send any other notifications.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: sh@shaz.app
