an android music player for your own music folder
 <img style="float: right; height: 8em;" src="img/icon.png">
 `bg1`SHazTunez` plays the mp3, flac, ogg n wav files you already
 have, straight from one folder on your phone.
No accounts, no streaming service, no ads.

it's open <a href="?pg=2">source :)</a>


<h3>get it</h3>
 - google play - coming soon

 - or build it from the <a pop href="https://github.com/Stephen-Hazel/tunez">source</a>


<h3>what it does</h3>
 - `bg2`rate` your songs your own way - the rating is just the end of the
   file name, so re-rating a song is a rename

 - `bg3`shuf` mixes all the ratings you check n won't repeat a song
   till you've heard em all

 - `bg4`cast` to a chromecast on your wifi

 - lock screen n headphone controls, album art, lyrics lookup
