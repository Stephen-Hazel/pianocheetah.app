naming your songs
Put your songs in Music/tunez on your phone (mp3, flac, ogg or wav).
 Subfolders are fine - their names show up next to the song.

<h3>file names</h3>
<b>artist &#96; year album track# &#96; title &#96; rating.mp3</b>

 - the middle part is optional

 - your subfolder name gets tacked onto the middle part

 - the rating is whatever's after the last &#96; - any short word you
   like.  every rating on your songs becomes a checkbox at the top

 - songs named any other way get tidied into that form for you, with
   the rating `bg2`new` so you can rate em later


<h3>ratings</h3>
ours are
 - `bg1`a` - songs we both like n could hear over n over

 - `bg2`b` - songs we both like but once max please

 - `bg3`aAn` `bg3`bAn` - annie's songs (that i don't like)

 - `bg4`aSt` `bg4`bSt` - steve's songs (that she doesn't like)

Keep em short or they use up tons of room at the top of the app.
